Add to Home Screen

Open Symplist in your browser and add it to your Home Screen for quick access.

iPhone & iPad
  1. Open Symplist in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow)
  3. Tap “Add to Home Screen”
  4. Tap “Add”
Android
  1. Open Symplist in Chrome
  2. Tap the menu button (⋮) in the top right
  3. Tap “Add to Home screen”
  4. Tap “Add”
